<Aside title="In progress">
  The worked examples are being written. This page lists which persona will illustrate each concept, and why.
</Aside>

Every example comes from a persona built on Gabriel Operator, so each one shows the format carrying real work.

| Concept | Persona | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontology | **Boli**, courses for ages 9 to 18 | A domain model of 32 entities. |
| Data feeds | **Archer**, marketing | 24 feeds, each with its own definition and source workflow. |
| Playbooks | **Archer**, marketing | Eight workflows, from a campaign journey to a cinematic ad. |
| Approval before an effect | **Paya**, online shopping | Simulates the purchase, shows the exact plan and buys only after approval. |
| Rehearsal | **Sherma**, form operations | Rehearses a form fill against a copy of the form before touching the real one. |
| Rehearsal | **Emil**, logistics | Rehearses every customs and carrier portal run before a human-approved action. |
| Pipelines | **Nest**, real estate | Three state machines: introductions, buyer property leads and seller leads. |
| Signals | **Lina**, recruiting | Watches public hiring signals and drafts outreach for a person to approve. |
| Teams | **Lane**, introductions | Two teams, one with six agents, and 17 playbooks. |
| Evals | **Boli**, **Chakri**, **Nest** | 36, 30 and 29 cases tied to stated requirements. |
| A governed domain | **Nora**, banking | Know-your-customer, credit and compliance work under explicit rules. |

## What each example will contain

1. The definition files, trimmed to the part that matters.
2. What a runtime does with them.
3. The eval cases that verify the behaviour.
4. What changes when the persona is imported into a second workspace.
