## Tour Playbook Studio

Open a persona in Gabriel and choose a team. Studio shows the team in up to ten tabs. Each view combines definitions from the package with live data from the workspace.

Choose a tab to see what people do there, what it reads from the folder, and which part of the specification covers it.

## The team behind the tour

The tour uses Lane. Its folder holds a library of 17 playbooks and two teams. The tour shows the second team, **Prospect to pilot**.

<Hierarchy />

**Prospect to pilot** has seven agents. Each runs one playbook from the library, and each declares the services it may call:

| Agent | Needs | Steps that wait for approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead researcher | `exa`: search | None declared |
| Outreach coordinator | `gmail`: send, reply, reply-status | Send |
| Meeting scheduler | `googlecalendar`: availability, book | None declared |
| Meeting scribe | `recall`: schedule-consented, completed-transcript | Capture |
| Follow-up writer | `gmail`: send, reply, reply-status | Send |
| Onboarding reviewer | Nothing | None declared |
| CRM steward | `notion`: update, through the team's app | Notion update |

The first six hand work to each other in order. Each of them also delegates to the CRM steward after every milestone, so the record is kept up to date as the journey goes.

The team's one app, **Pilot prospects**, is a grid over the list `list.lane.prospect-pilot`. Its pipeline has 12 stages, from Identified through Contacted, Meeting booked and Onboarding review to Converted, plus Dead and Needs you.

## Working together

Studio is built for several people and several agents on one team at the same time.

- **Presence.** Everyone working in the team shows as an avatar, and on the canvas as a cursor with their name. A click shows as a ripple in their colour.
- **Following.** Choose someone's avatar to follow them. Your screen shows what theirs shows: the same tab, the same agent or page open, the same part of the canvas at the same zoom. Following only shows; it never starts anything for you, and your own access still applies.
- **The team conversation.** One conversation per team, beside every tab. People talk to each other; an agent answers only when it is tagged. Pictures can be attached or pasted.
- **Calls and meetings.** A call puts the team in as a voice. It answers when someone calls it by name, asks a playbook to answer in its own voice, and rings people in. Anyone in the call can take the team out and bring it back. Meetings have their own call and their own conversation.
- **Shared pages.** Pages in Context are edited together, live. What people write and what agents maintain stay apart.
- **Agents as colleagues.** Everything Studio does is also a tool on the package's server. The Coach, Gabriel's assistant beside every page, and any connected agent can create a team, add a agent, start a goal or read why a agent is blocked, with the same permissions and the same approvals as a person clicking.

The format covers the parts these features read: teams, playbooks, requirements, lists and apps. [Apps and connectors](../apps/) specifies them, together with the event stream that keeps every open view current and the shape of presence.
