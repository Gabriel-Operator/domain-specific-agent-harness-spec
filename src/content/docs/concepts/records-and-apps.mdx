---
title: Records, pipelines, and apps
description: How connectors and agents publish shared data and how a team app displays it.
---

Connector results and agent outputs become shared work when a playbook publishes selected fields into a list. A **list** defines records and fields. A **pipeline** defines the stages and transitions of a record. An **app** renders those records for the team and can let a person act on them.

## Follow one record

1. The research playbook reads evidence about Northwind from a connector and maps approved fields for publication.
2. The outreach playbook sends the reviewed message and publishes the outcome. The prospect record moves to Contacted.
3. Pilot prospects displays the record from the same list. With live sync, open views receive the change. A configured stage transition can start another permitted playbook.

A publish step declares which fields may leave the private run. A pipeline transition governs a change of state, while a connector operation governs an external effect. Both can affect what a person sees in the app.

## Built-in views and custom apps

A list-backed grid or board is a declarative view. Its definition names the list, fields, and optional pipeline. A custom app package has its own manifest and interface. A workspace installation binds the package to permitted resources and connector accounts. The package can be versioned independently of the team that installs it.
