The **vault** holds the credentials that a workspace uses to bind connector requirements to authenticated accounts. A persona declares logical requirements and references. The workspace supplies the specific account and its grants.

## Account scope

A personal connection uses the acting member’s account. A shared connection is set up for a team resource with its own grants. For example, Lane can use a person’s Gmail account for outreach and a shared Notion destination for the team’s app. Copying a playbook to another workspace requires configuring the connections there.

A hosted custom app uses its authorized app bridge for operations. The vault keeps the underlying provider credentials behind that connection.
