A **signal** evaluates an observation against a condition. The signal gives the team a reason to act or to look closer. A data feed supplies the observation, and a playbook carries out any work the team decides to start.

## Example

A recruiting feed sees that a company announced a role. A signal can flag the opening when it meets the team’s criteria. A playbook then qualifies the company and prepares outreach. Sending still depends on the authorized account and the relevant approval.

Gabriel keeps observations, enabled schedules, and outcomes in the workspace. A portable contract for signals and schedules is planned.
