A **signal** evaluates an observation against a condition. The signal gives the team a reason to act or to look closer. A data feed supplies the observation, and a playbook carries out any work the team decides to start.

## Evaluate a condition

### Observation, rule, and eligibility

Record the source and observation ID before evaluating the team’s rule. A condition should make clear which data must be present and which cases should be held. A matched observation can propose work. The workspace decides whether the trigger is enabled, the destination team is eligible, and the occurrence has already been handled.

### Example hiring signal

This is decision logic for an implementation. The draft leaves the portable signal schema open.

```text
Observe a dated hiring announcement with a source URL.
If company identity or announcement date is ambiguous, hold for specialist review.
If the announcement is new and matches the team’s criteria, propose a research goal.
If its occurrence was already handled, link to the existing outcome.
```

## Trace the outcome

Keep the observation, the matched rule, any human decision, and the resulting goal or run together by reference. A later revision can change the rule and be checked against a replayed fixture. The existing outcome remains evidence of what happened under the prior revision.
