**Binding** connects a portable requirement to a resource available in an installation. A definition can request structured model output, a calendar read, or an approver role. The workspace supplies a compatible model, an authorized calendar account, or the person assigned to review.

## Resolve and check access

Before dispatch, check that the selected resource can perform the operation and that the acting member or team has the necessary grant. A missing binding holds the affected work. A published definition can be reused in another workspace with different bindings.

In Meeting desk, `calendar:upcoming` is a logical operation. The installation binds it to a member-authorized calendar and maps the returned data to the meetings field. A separate publisher binding identifies where an approved summary may go.
