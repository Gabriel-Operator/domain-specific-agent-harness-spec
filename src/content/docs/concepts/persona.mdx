A **persona** is the Git-backed definition that a runtime imports. It names the domain, describes the work its teams can perform, and brings together their playbooks, data definitions, and review expectations.

## What ships with a persona

Start with the root `SKILL.md` and the published `assets/chat-config.json`. Add ontology, eval, and Studio assets for the features the persona uses. The portable registry pins linked list, pipeline, and workflow definitions. A workspace binds the imported revision to local accounts and stores its records and run history.

## Example

Lane is a persona for introductions and pilots. One team can research prospects and prepare outreach. Another can reuse a reviewed playbook for a different goal. Each workspace supplies its own access and keeps its own outcomes.
