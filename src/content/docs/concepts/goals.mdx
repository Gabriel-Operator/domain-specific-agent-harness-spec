A **goal** describes the outcome a team should produce and how to judge completion. A goal belongs to a team. Its plan selects available playbooks and orders their work according to dependencies and the requested outcome.

## Purpose, reusable planning, and a live goal

The `goal` field on a team describes its enduring purpose. A saved playbook with `kind: "goal"` defines reusable planning with allowed capabilities and limits. When a person starts work, the workspace holds the specific goal, its inputs, plan, and execution history.

If an Accountants goal needs evidence and a first draft, it can invoke research and writing. A missing capability can lead an author to draft and register a playbook for review before adding it to a plan.
