A **team** groups agents and resources around a domain responsibility. One persona can declare several teams. A team owns the goals started inside it, the agents available for those goals, and shared definitions such as boards and lists.

## How a team is defined

In `assets/studio-teams.json`, a team declares an ID, a purpose in `goal`, a revision, a budget, placements in `bots`, and handoff edges in `connections`. The handoff edges link placements inside that team. Each placement pins the playbook it runs.

The Accountants team can place Lead Finder, Signal Researcher, Copywriter, and Sequence Reviewer. A qualification goal may need only the first two. A goal that includes outreach can also select the writing and review placements.
