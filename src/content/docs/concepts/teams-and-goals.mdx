A **team** owns the agents and shared resources that do a particular job. A **goal** describes an outcome and what counts as done. A **playbook** defines reusable work, including its inputs, required connector operations, and permitted outputs. A **run** is one attempt to carry out that work.

## From an outcome to a plan

In Lane, a person gives Prospect to pilot a goal to qualify Northwind and prepare a follow-up. Research fit and Follow up are two reusable playbooks. A plan can use both and give each agent the evidence it needs for the next step. A team author can also draft a playbook from an outcome and register it after review.

Gabriel distinguishes **Build from steps**, where the author specifies a sequence or graph of steps, from **Plan from a goal**, where the author supplies an objective, completion criteria, permitted capabilities, and limits for planning. Registration saves the definition. Starting work is a separate action that checks the selected plan and current access.

## Canvas and Work

| Surface | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Canvas** | Team agents, handoffs, context, and the registered playbook library. An agent placement references a playbook. |
| **Work** | Goals, their plans and tasks, execution settings, and progress from planning to completion. |
| **Active tasks** | Running or waiting work, with links back to the goal and any approval it needs. |
