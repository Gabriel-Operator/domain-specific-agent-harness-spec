The proposed **marketplace** makes playbooks discoverable across teams and personas. Authors can also list complete personas that combine reviewed definitions around a particular domain.

## Adopt a playbook

Review its revision and check that the destination team can supply the inputs, map the outputs, and grant its required operations. A Research playbook can pair with Verify in one persona and Draft in another. Each workspace uses its own accounts and approvals.

Marketplace listing and cross-runtime installation formats are planned. Gabriel’s custom app registry has its own implemented package and installation contract.

See [playbook reuse](../teams-and-goals/#reuse-a-playbook) and [the planned marketplace contract](../../specification/#8-not-yet-specified-).
