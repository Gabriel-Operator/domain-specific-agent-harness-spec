**Human review** is a decision made by someone with the authority the playbook needs. A domain specialist can settle a disputed match or correct a proposed plan. An authorized approver decides whether a protected action may take effect.

## What gets approved

Reviewing the scope lets preparation continue. Approving a protected effect covers its exact target, operation, inputs, acting account, and policy. The runtime holds dependent work until the right person decides. A material change calls for a fresh proposal and approval.

A specialist may decide which of two prospects is the right record. A subsequent email still needs the configured send approval for the specific recipient and message.
