A **list** defines the fields that a team uses to store and display records. A **record** is one live entry, such as a prospect with a name, status, and last contact date. An app can show records through a grid or board.

## Publish a record

A playbook can publish selected output fields through a named publisher. The workspace binds that publisher to a list and field allow-list, and checks destination access, source grants, and applicable consent. List definitions travel with a persona. Live rows stay in the workspace.
