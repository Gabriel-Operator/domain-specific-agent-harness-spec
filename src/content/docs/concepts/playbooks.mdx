A **playbook** describes work a team can invoke again. The generic term is workflow. A Studio playbook declares a stable key, revision, inputs, connector requirements, and named outputs. A team placement can refer to a reviewed revision.

## Structured and goal playbooks

A structured playbook gives the runtime steps and dependencies. Those steps can reason, use connectors, [invoke another playbook](../delegation/), [wait](../scheduled-work/), or publish. A goal playbook supplies an objective and completion criteria along with the capabilities and limits allowed for planning.

For Meeting desk, one playbook reads an authorized calendar and prepares a summary. Another reviews and publishes the summary to a team list. The destination workspace supplies the account and publisher grant.
