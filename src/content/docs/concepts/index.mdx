The reference is organized around what you define, what runs, and what a team can see afterward. Each topic connects the Gabriel Operator implementation to the relevant part of the draft. The [implementation guide](../implementation/) links each architecture component to its files or workspace behavior. Draft rules live in the [Specification](../specification/) and the [Apps and connectors profile](../apps/). Studio behavior and skills illustrate how Gabriel implements those ideas.

<div class="ap-topic-index">

| Topic | Where you encounter it in Gabriel Operator |
| --- | --- |
| [Personas and workspaces](./personas-and-workspaces/) | Create or import a persona, change its definitions, and open an installed workspace. |
| [Teams, goals, and playbooks](./teams-and-goals/) | Canvas shows agents and the playbook library. Work holds goals and tasks. |
| [Governed execution](./governed-execution/) | Human decisions, isolated rehearsals, exact action approval, and verified execution. |
| [Connectors and approvals](./connectors-and-approvals/) | Connectors shows required operations and setup. Vault protects credentials. Approvals shows the pending decisions. |
| [Records, pipelines, and apps](./records-and-apps/) | Apps displays shared records and the stages those records move through. |
| [Runs and collaboration](./runs-and-collaboration/) | Context, Conversations, Outputs, and Activity show the work around a run and the evidence it left. |
| [Signals, evidence, and evals](./signals-and-evals/) | Data feeds supply observations. Specialists review anomalies and turn decisions into proposed rules and evals. |

</div>
